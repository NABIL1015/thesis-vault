## Reading note

Only pages 1110-1111 matter for a 2D airfoil. Pages 1111-1113 are a
slender delta wing with vortex breakdown; 1113-1114 is a full aircraft.

The tau_1 of roughly 15 c/V that appears in the delta-wing section is
**not** an airfoil value and should not be cited as one. The airfoil
values are tau_1 = 0.52 c/V, tau_2 = 4.5 c/V, from ramp motions on a
NACA 0015 (Fig. 2).
